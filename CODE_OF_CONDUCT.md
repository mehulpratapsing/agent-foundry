# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in Agent Foundry to be welcoming, respectful, and focused on improving the toolkit. Be considerate of differing experience levels, identities, backgrounds, and viewpoints. Give feedback about ideas and code, not personal worth.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, discriminatory or sexualized language, personal attacks, doxxing, threats, and sustained disruption of a discussion.

## Maintainer response

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to keep the community safe. They will consider the context and impact of a report and aim to respond proportionately.

## Reporting

Contact a repository maintainer privately with a description and, when safe, links or other relevant context. Do not publish private information about another person in an issue or pull request.
